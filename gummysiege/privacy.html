Privacy Policy

Gummy Siege: Candy Crowd Battle, by Ramo Labs. Effective 11 November 2026.

This policy explains what information the game Gummy Siege ("the game", "we") uses, and why. The game doesn't ask for your name, email address or any account, and we don't run our own servers.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, kingdom buildings, cards, chests, daily quests and gifts) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved only on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this progress in your device backup so you can restore it on a new phone. That backup is managed by Google under your device settings, and we never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads using Google AdMob: a banner on the kingdom screen, an ad after some battles, an ad when you return to the game, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward (for example double coins, a second chance in a battle, or a bigger chest). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address;
  • information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't receive information from Google that identifies you. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Your choices in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for advertising. You can change your choice at any time: open the game's Settings (the gear button on the kingdom screen) and tap Privacy choices.

No purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. Coins, chests and cards are earned only by playing, and they have no real-world value.

Ratings and reviews

The game may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Any rating or review you submit is handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teens.

Security and retention

We don't collect or store your personal information on any server of our own. Ad requests are sent over encrypted connections, and data processed by Google is kept and protected according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
