Privacy Policy

Depot Rush: Parcel Idle, by Ramo Labs. Effective 3 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the game Depot Rush ("the game", "we") uses, and why. The game doesn't ask for your name, email address or any account, and we don't run our own servers.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (money, unlocks, upgrades, daily rewards), your settings (sound, vibration) and whether you bought "Remove ads" are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Some ads are optional videos you choose to watch for a reward; players who haven't bought "Remove ads" may also see an ad between Rush Orders. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address;
  • information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Your choices in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time: open the game's Settings (the gear button) and tap Privacy settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own the purchase, so it can switch the ads off and restore your purchase if you reinstall. Google's handling of payment information is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security and retention

We don't collect or store your personal information on any server of our own. Data processed by Google is kept and protected according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
